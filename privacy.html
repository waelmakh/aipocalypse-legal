Privacy Policy

AIpocalypse · Last updated 2026-08-05

This policy explains how AIpocalypse handles your information. AIpocalypse is provided by Wael Makhoul.

We do not collect your data

AIpocalypse does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking.

Content you create in the app — notes, saved items, settings, and any documents you add — stays on your device. We cannot read it, and it is never uploaded to us.

Network connections

AIpocalypse works offline. When you choose to download optional content, the app contacts a content server to retrieve the files you asked for. These requests carry the technical information every internet request carries, such as your device's IP address. We do not use this to build a profile of you.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active. Apple's handling of that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them. Each is used on your device and the resulting data is not transmitted to us.

  • Camera — to photograph plants or objects for on-device identification
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — for voice input to the assistant
  • Location — to centre offline maps and show your compass heading
  • Face ID — to unlock your document vault

Children

AIpocalypse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
