Privacy Policy

AIpocalypse · Last updated 2026-08-09

This policy explains how AIpocalypse handles your information. AIpocalypse is provided by Wael Makhoul.

Your content stays on your device

AIpocalypse has no account system and no server that holds your content. Everything you create or store in the app — your questions to the assistant and its answers, notes, saved items, vault documents, map positions, and settings — stays on your device. We cannot read any of it, and it is never uploaded to us.

The AI assistant runs on your device. Your questions are answered by a language model that has been downloaded onto your phone, so the text of a question is not sent to us or to any AI service.

There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no tracking identifiers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites. Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

What leaves your device

  • Downloads. AIpocalypse works offline, but the maps, reference content, and AI model files you choose to download are fetched over the internet — from a content server we operate and from Hugging Face, which hosts the model files. Like any internet request, these carry technical information such as your device's IP address, which the server hosting the file necessarily sees. We do not use this to build a profile of you, and none of your content is included in those requests.
  • Purchases (Apple). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of the transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat). We use RevenueCat to manage and report on purchases. When you launch the app or make a purchase, the app shares anonymous purchase information — the product purchased, whether it is active, and an anonymous, randomly generated identifier — with RevenueCat, so the app knows to unlock Pro. That identifier is not your name or your email, and it is not used for advertising. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Dictation, if you use it. Speech recognition is performed on your device wherever your device supports it. On a device or in a language where on-device recognition is not available, iOS may send the audio of what you dictate to Apple for processing, under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can avoid this entirely by typing instead of dictating.

Device permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the feature that needs them. Except for dictation as described above, each is used on your device and the resulting data is not transmitted to us.

  • Camera — to scan medicine labels and barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — to dictate a question to the assistant.
  • Location — to show your position on offline maps and your heading on the compass. Your location is used on the device and is never sent to us.
  • Face ID — only to unlock your encrypted document vault, handled entirely by iOS. Your biometric data never reaches AIpocalypse.

Keeping and deleting your information

Because everything lives on your device, you control it. Deleting an item in the app removes it, and deleting the app removes everything AIpocalypse stored, including any downloaded maps, content, and model files. There is no account for us to delete and no request you need to send us. A purchase belongs to your Apple Account and is not affected by deleting the app; you can restore it later.

Children

AIpocalypse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
